Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

1. Who processes your data

NAVIA (Ivan DUBROVKO, French micro-entrepreneur, SIREN 108 736 406 — see legal notice), is responsible for processing data collected through this site. Contact: naviaproduction@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

When you submit a request through the site's form, we collect: your answers to the questionnaire (occasion, information about the person being celebrated, style and concept preferences), along with your name, email and phone number. No photo is requested at this stage.

Once the order is confirmed, further exchanges (email or WhatsApp) are used to collect 3 good-quality photos needed to produce the video.

3. Purpose of processing

This data is used exclusively to: assess and price your request, produce the ordered video, communicate with you during production, and manage the business relationship (invoicing, follow-up).

4. Legal basis

Processing is based on pre-contractual steps taken at your request, then on the performance of the service contract once the order is confirmed.

5. Recipients and hosting

The request form is submitted via Netlify Forms (Netlify, Inc., USA) and forwarded by email and WhatsApp to the provider. Photos, produced videos and order history are kept by NAVIA on infrastructure located in the European Union. This data is never sold or shared with third parties for commercial purposes.

6. Retention period

If you agree to let your video be used as a portfolio example, you get a 10% discount on your order, and the project may be kept beyond the current year for showcase purposes. If you decline, your video, order history and photos are kept until the end of the current calendar year, then permanently deleted. If you place a new order after that date, this information and the photos will need to be provided again.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to the processing of your data, as well as the right to data portability. To exercise these rights, contact us at naviaproduction@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL, www.cnil.fr).

8. Security

Reasonable measures are implemented to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss or disclosure.

9. Cookies and audience measurement

This site uses no tracking or advertising cookies, and sets no identifier that would let us recognise you or follow you across other websites.

Site traffic is measured with Umami, a cookieless analytics tool. What is recorded, in aggregate form: the pages viewed, the referring page or search engine, the country, the language, the device type and browser, and interactions with the page (button clicks, reading progress, time spent). Your IP address is not stored, no advertising identifier is created, and no individual profile is built.

These statistics are used solely to understand what visitors are interested in and to improve the site. They are neither sold nor passed on to third parties for commercial purposes, and are kept for thirteen months at most. Because this measurement is strictly limited to improving the site and cannot identify you, it does not require prior consent. You may nevertheless block it at any time using a blocking extension in your browser.
